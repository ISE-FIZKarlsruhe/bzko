## Lisa: "Which widows applied on behalf of their persecuted husbands?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | Lisa (P06) |
| **Role** | Master student in Gender Studies |
| **Goal** | She wants to research the compensation proceedings of widowed women applying for their persecuted husband. |
| **Research scenario** | She searches in TP WGM with the filters "marital status", "gender" and "role" to find out evidence of the persons fitting her research topic. |
| **User story** | **US1** As a master's student in Gender Studies, I want to filter records by gender and role, so that I can find evidence of women applying for compensation on behalf of a persecuted husband. |
| **Competency questions** | **P06-CQ1** Which widowed women applied for compensation for their persecuted husbands? ⚠️<br>**P06-CQ2** How many widowed women applied for compensation for their persecuted husbands? ⚠️ |
| **Notes** | Not all BZK cards contain information about marital status or familial relationships, and this information has not been extracted. Moreover, gender is neither represented in the data schema nor explicitly recorded on the cards. Finally, we cannot determine the relationship between a widow and men with the same surname, as a widow may have applied for compensation on behalf of her husband, son, father-in-law, or brother-in-law. Therefore, these questions cannot be answered based on the BZK cards or the BZK KG. |
