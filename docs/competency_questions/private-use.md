## John: "Who are the heirs, my relatives in Europe?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | John (P02) |
| **Role** | Distant relative of a persecuted person |
| **Goal** | His own grandparents emigrated to the USA; he is not familiar with the family branch closer to the persecuted person, which has stayed in Europe. He is interested in renewing family bonds. With the help of the compensation files, he hopes to find out more about the heirs — his relatives. |
| **Research scenario** | He goes to the TP WGM and searches for the persecuted person, using name and birthdate as search items. The names of heirs may be available in the file or on the BZK card. |
| **User stories** | **US1** As a distant relative of a persecuted person, I want to search for that person by name and birthdate, so that I can find their compensation file.<br>**US2** As someone hoping to renew family bonds, I want to see whether heirs are named within a found file or on the BZK card, so that I can identify living relatives who stayed in Europe.<br>**US3** As a distant relative with limited family knowledge, I want to explore other names and places mentioned in the compensation record, so that I can piece together the family branch I have lost touch with. |
| **Competency questions** | **P02-CQ1** Did a person with a given name X, a family name Y, and a date of birth Z apply for compensation?<br>**P02-CQ2** Are any heirs named on the BZK card for a persecuted person with a given name X, a family name Y, and a date of birth Z? ⚠️ *knowledge gap* |
| **Notes** | **Knowledge gap (P02-CQ2):** Heirs are not a search criterion in itself in the Online Collection; however, BZK KG can answer this query. |

## Stolperstein-Initiative Rheinhausen: "Who lived in Rheinhausen, and where are their records?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | Stolperstein-Initiative Rheinhausen (P03) |
| **Role** | Local remembrance initiative |
| **Goal** | **Goal 1:** The initiative wants to place "tumbling stones" in Rheinhausen to commemorate persecuted persons at the last place that they chose freely to reside, work, or study. They need compensation records to find out more about their fate during National Socialism.<br><br>**Goal 2:** The initiative wants to place a "tumbling stone" for a particular person or a family. They need compensation records to find out more about their fate during National Socialism. |
| **Research scenario** | **Scenario 1:** The initiative goes to the TP WGM and searches for all persecuted persons with "Rheinhausen" or a particular street in Rheinhausen as their address. They find out whether there is a compensation record and where it is kept.<br><br>**Scenario 2:** The initiative goes to the TP WGM and searches for the names and birth dates of the persons and, if available, also for the birthplace and place of residence prior to persecution. They also check the deathplaces, which include Rheinhausen. They find out whether there is a compensation record and where it is kept. |
| **User stories** | **US1** As a remembrance initiative, I want to search for persecuted people connected with Rheinhausen and locate their records so that I can identify candidates for a Stolperstein at that location.<br>**US2** As a remembrance initiative, I want to search by name and date of birth (and birthplace if known), so that I can find out whether a specific person or family has a compensation record.<br>**US3** As a remembrance initiative, I want to search persons whose deathplace is Rheinhausen.<br>**US4** As a remembrance initiative, I want to see where a found compensation record is archived, so that I can request access to it for my research. |
| **Competency questions** | **P03-CQ1** Which persecuted persons have street/city X as part of their address?<br>**P03-CQ2** Did a person with a given name X, a family name Y, a date of birth Z, and, if available, a birthplace W apply for compensation?<br>**P03-CQ3** Which persons' deathplace is city/town X?<br>**P03-CQ4** In which archive is the BZK card for a person with the given name X and family name Y kept?|

## Markus: "Is there evidence of my grandfather's German citizenship?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | Markus (P04) |
| **Role** | Decendant of a person who emigrated to the USA in 1937 due to persecution and became an American citizen |
| **Goal** | He wants to find out whether his grandfather applied for compensation and find out if there is evidence for the German citizenship of his grandfather in the compensation record. |
| **Research scenario** | He goes to the TP WGM and searches for his grandfather's name, date of birth, birthplace, and his last address to check whether a compensation application exists. |
| **User stories** | **US1** As a descendant of an emigrant, I want to search by name, date of birth, birthplace, and last known address, so that I can check whether a compensation application exists for my grandfather.<br>**US2** As a citizenship applicant's descendant, I want to see whether a compensation record contains evidence of German citizenship, so that I can check for evidence relevant to citizenship. |
| **Competency questions** | **P04-CQ1** Which BZK cards are associated with a persecuted person whose given name is X, family name is Y, date of birth is Z, birthplace is W, and address is H?<br>**P04-CQ2** Which BZK cards are associated with a persecuted person whose given name or alternative given name is X?<br>**P04-CQ3** Which BZK cards are associated with a persecuted person whose family name or alternative family name is Y? |
| **Notes** | Not all BZK cards contain citizenship information. Moreover, citizenship information has not been extracted and is therefore not represented in BZKO. The existence of a BZK card for a persecuted person born in Germany can, however, serve as evidence. |
