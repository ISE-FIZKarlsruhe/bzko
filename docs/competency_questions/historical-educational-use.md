## Ludger: "How did National Socialism affect Schwielow/Elbe?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | Ludger (P14) |
| **Role** | Member of a local history association of Schwielow/Elbe |
| **Goal** | He wants to document how National Socialism affected Schwielow/Elbe for a local chronicle covering the years 1933–1945 and the period since then. He knows of several Jewish families, organizations, and communities that were based there. |
| **Research scenario** | He browses TP WGM using the filters "birthplace", "place of residence prior to persecution", "place of residence at time of application", and "reason for persecution" for private persons. For organizations, he uses the filters "location" and "reason for persecution". The filter "reason for persecution" does not apply to the BZK cards. |
| **User stories** | **US1** As a local historian, I want to browse compensation records filtered by birthplace and places of residence, so that I can understand and document the reasons for the persecution that took place in Schwielow/Elbe.<br>**US2** As a local historian, I want to filter organizations by location, so that I can identify Jewish organizations and communities based in Schwielow/Elbe and the reasons for their persecution. |
| **Competency questions** | **P14-CQ1** Which persecuted persons have a birthplace or address in city X?<br>**P14-CQ2** What are the reasons for the persecution of people whose birthplace or address is in city X? ⚠️<br>**P14-CQ3** Which organizations were located in city/town X?<br>**P14-CQ4** What are the reasons for the persecution of organizations located in city X? ⚠️<br>**P14-CQ5** Which organizations located in X match the keywords "Jüdischer Verein" or "Jüdische Organisation" in their name? ⚠️ |
| **Notes** | **Available elsewhere in the WGM collection (P14-CQ2, P14-CQ4):** Reasons for the persecution of persons and organizations are not recorded on the BZK cards; they can be found in other documents of the WGM collection.<br><br>**Available in the BZK KG (P14-CQ5):** This query can be answered in BZK KG. |

## Mr. H. Storian: "Who applied for whom, and through which office?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | Mr. H. Storian (P16) |
| **Role** | Historian of post-war Germany's relationship with its past |
| **Goal** | **Goal 1:** Interested in first-hand compensation, he wants to know: (1) how many persecuted persons applied for themselves and, in contrast, how many applications were filed on behalf of someone else; (2) which individuals or entities filed applications for non-relatives; (3) how many applicants did not apply for themselves but for relatives; (4) the number of applications per compensation office.<br><br>**Goal 2:** Interested in the work of organizations in the field, he wants (1) to identify the relevant organizations and (2) to gain insights into the cases these organizations worked on. |
| **Research scenario** | **Scenario 1:** He queries BZK KG for (1) cards that have no applicant information and cards that have both an applicant and a persecuted person; (2) cards with applicant and persecuted-person details and different surnames; (3) cards with applicant and persecuted-person details and identical surnames; and (4) the number of applications per compensation office.<br><br>**Scenario 2:** He queries BZK KG for (1) cards with persecuted persons and applicants, filtering for applicants with more than X applications, and (2) cards with organizations as applicants. |
| **User stories** | **US1** As a historian of post-war Germany, I want to query cards with no applicant information versus cards with both an applicant and a persecutee, so that I can determine how many persecuted persons applied for themselves versus through someone else.<br>**US2** As a historian, I want to compare surnames of applicants and persecuted persons on the same card, so that I can distinguish relatives from non-relatives filing applications.<br>**US3** As a historian, I want to filter cards by the number of applications per compensation office, so that I can analyze processing patterns across offices.<br>**US4** As a historian, I want to identify organizations listed as applicants and the number of applications each filed, so that I can study the role organizations played in compensation cases. |
| **Competency questions** | **P16-CQ1** How many BZK cards have no person with an applicant role?<br>**P16-CQ2** How many BZK cards are associated with two distinct people, one with an applicant role and the other with a persecutee role?<br>**P16-CQ3** How many BZK cards are associated with two distinct people, one with an applicant role and the other with a persecutee role, who have the same family name? ⚠️<br>**P16-CQ4** How many BZK cards are associated with distinct compensation offices?<br>**P16-CQ5** Which organizations have an applicant role?<br>**P16-CQ6** How many BZK cards are associated with distinct organizations that have an applicant role? |
| **Notes** | **Knowledge gap (P16-CQ3):** A shared family name does not necessarily indicate a familial relationship, and relatives may have different family names (e.g., aunt and nephew). Since familial relationships do not necessarily appear on the cards, and those that do appear have not been extracted, this CQ only compares family names and cannot distinguish relatives from non-relatives. |

## Petra: "Was a claim ever filed for this athlete?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona name** | Petra (P19) |
| **Role** | Investigative journalist working for a renowned magazine |
| **Goal** | She is researching a story about a famous athlete who initially supported, and was supported by, the Nazi regime, only to be persecuted and killed later. She does not know whether he had any surviving relatives or heirs. To gather material for her story, she is looking for records of compensation proceedings that may have been initiated on his behalf. |
| **Research scenario** | She browses TP WGM, entering his name, date and place of birth, and date of death if at hand. She might filter by the role "persecuted" to narrow down the results. |
| **User stories** | **US1** As an investigative journalist, I want to search by name, birth date, birthplace, and death date, so that I can find out whether a compensation proceeding was filed for this historical figure.<br>**US2** As an investigative journalist, I want to filter by the role "persecuted", so that I can narrow down results among possible name matches. |
| **Competency questions** | **P19-CQ1** Which BZK cards are associated with people whose given name is X, family name is Y, date of birth is Z, birthplace is W, and date of death is H?<br>**P19-CQ2** Which BZK cards are associated with people whose given or alternative given name is X and family or alternative family name is Y?<br>**P19-CQ3** Who are the heirs of the persecuted person whose given name is X and family name is Y? ⚠️ |
| **Notes** | **Available in the BZK KG (P19-CQ3):** Heirs are not a search criterion in TP WGM; however, BZK KG can answer this query. |
